经过前面的推导我们不难发现，构造 SDE 是很简单的，但是从 SDE 构造$p(x_t|x_0)$是不容易的。那么，我们不妨定义$p(x_t|x_0)$，再去推导对应的 SDE
定义

$$p(x_t|x_0)=N(x_t:\bar{\alpha}_tx_0,\bar{\beta}_tI)$$
我们把$x_0$到$x_{t+\Delta t}$的过程分为两部分：$x_0\to x_t,\quad x_t\to x_{t+\Delta t}$
$$
\begin{equation}  
p(x_{t+\Delta t}\mid x_0)  
=  
\int p(x_{t+\Delta t}\mid x_t)\,p(x_t\mid x_0)\,dx_t  
\end{equation}
$$
SDE 离散形式推导
$$
\begin{equation}  
\begin{aligned}  
x_{t+\Delta t}  
&= (1+f_t\Delta t)\,x_t + g_t\sqrt{\Delta t}\,\epsilon_2 \\  
&= (1+f_t\Delta t)\bigl(\bar{\alpha}_t x_0 + \bar{\beta}_t \epsilon_1\bigr)  
+ g_t\sqrt{\Delta t}\,\epsilon_2 \\  
&= (1+f_t\Delta t)\bar{\alpha}_t x_0  
+ \Bigl((1+f_t\Delta t)\bar{\beta}_t\epsilon_1 + g_t\sqrt{\Delta t}\,\epsilon_2\Bigr)  
\end{aligned}  
\end{equation}
$$
所以
$$
\begin{align}  
\bar{\alpha}_{t+\Delta t}  
&= (1+f_t\Delta t)\bar{\alpha}_t \\  
\bar{\beta}_{t+\Delta t}^{\,2}  
&= (1+f_t\Delta t)^2 \bar{\beta}_t^{\,2} + g_t^2 \Delta t  
\end{align}
$$
求$\Delta t \to 0$的极限
$$
\begin{equation}  
f_t=\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(\ln \bar{\alpha}_t\bigr)  
=\frac{1}{\bar{\alpha}_t}\frac{d\bar{\alpha}_t}{dt},  
\qquad  
g_t^2=\bar{\alpha}_t^{\,2}\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\bar{\beta}_t^{\,2}}{\bar{\alpha}_t^{\,2}}\right)  
=2\bar{\alpha}_t\bar{\beta}_t\,\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\bar{\beta}_t}{\bar{\alpha}_t}\right)  
\end{equation}
$$
当$\bar{\alpha}_t=1$时，就是 VE-SDE，当$\bar{\alpha}_t^2+\bar{\beta}_t^2=1$时，就是 VP-SDE，至于 loss ，此时算得
$$
\begin{equation}  
\nabla_{x_t}\log p(x_t\mid x_0)  
=  
-\frac{x_t-\bar{\alpha}_t x_0}{\bar{\beta}_t^{\,2}}  
=  
-\frac{\epsilon}{\bar{\beta}_t}  
\end{equation}
$$
